To pitch your services to an Instagram seller or professional, you need a strategy that breaks through the noise of their busy DMs. To show them your value, you can pair your pitch with an automated portfolio tracker.

# Part 1: How to Pitch an Instagram Seller or Professional

Instagram professionals receive dozens of generic DMs a day. To stand out, personalize your approach and lead with value rather than a sales pitch.
• Interact before you pitch: Follow their account, engage with their recent posts, and reply thoughtfully to their Stories. This makes your name familiar before you ever slide into their DMs.
• Identify a specific gap: Look closely at their profile. Are their Reels missing clear captions? Is their link-in-bio broken or messy? Could their product photos be improved?
• Send a "Value-First" DM: Reach out by pointing out the gap gently and offering a quick, free solution or a piece of advice.
• Send a personalized video: Instead of a wall of text, send a 60-second Loom video or Instagram video DM. Walk through their page, show them what you can improve, and briefly show your portfolio.

# Part 2: Automated Portfolio Tools (Self-Updating)

If you want a website that keeps your work organized automatically without you having to manually upload files every day, you need a platform that integrates directly with the tools you already use (like Instagram, Google Drive, or GitHub).

Here are the best options based on your workflow:

Platform	Best For	How it Automates
Behance / Adobe Portfolio	Visual Creators & Designers	Can sync automatically with your Adobe Creative Cloud files or import projects instantly.

Bento.me	Content Creators & Social Managers	Links all your social feeds (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) into one live, auto-updating grid.

Polywork	Professionals & Freelancers	Automatically pulls in your latest achievements, articles, or project updates from external links.

Format or Squarespace	Photographers & Artists	Offers features to automatically sync and display images directly from an Instagram feed or Dropbox folder.

# How to build a fully custom automated portfolio:

If you want complete control, you can build a simple landing page using Notion (turned into a website via Super.so or Fruition). You can use automation tools like Zapier or Make.com to set up a trigger: "Every time I post on Instagram or add a file to a specific Google Drive folder, automatically add it as a new project row in my Notion portfolio website."
